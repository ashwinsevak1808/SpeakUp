Introduction: From Reading to Doing

You have read seventeen chapters. You know about mindset, pronunciation, grammar, conversations, interviews and much more. But reading about English is like reading about cycling: you only improve when you ride. This chapter gives you a ninety-day road map. It is simple, small and built for busy people. At the end you will sound and feel different.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • How the 30/60/90-day plan is organised
  • What to do each day and each week
  • How to track your progress honestly
  • How to recover if you miss days
  • How to celebrate and move to the next level

Rules of the plan

1. Never miss twice in a row. 2. Speak aloud every day. 3. Keep a tracker. 4. Review every Sunday. 5. Be kind to yourself.

Lesson 5Track, Review, Recover

The Sunday Review (10 minutes)

  1. Count how many days you practised (out of seven).
  2. Note one win and one difficulty.
  3. Pick one specific focus for the next week.

If you miss days

Life happens: exams, festivals, illness. Do not quit. Restart the next day with a short five-minute version. Your plan is a guide, not a punishment. In Hinglish: Ek din chhoot gaya toh kya hua, kal phir shuru karenge. In English: "I missed a day. No problem, I'm starting again today."

Sample dialogue · Accountability partners

SnehaHow was your week? Did you complete your 20 minutes?

AartiFive out of seven days. I missed Thursday and Friday because of my cousin's wedding.

SnehaThat's fine. Five days is a good start. What was your best moment?

AartiI spoke to a stranger at the station. I asked for directions in English!

SnehaBrilliant. Next week let's aim for six days.

Common Mistakes and Corrections

WrongRight
I am following the plan from last month.I've been following the plan for a month.
I missed the practice so I felt very guilty.I missed a day, but I'm back on track.
Today I have done my speaking practice.Today I did my speaking practice. / I've done my speaking practice today.
I will join the course from next Monday only.I'll start on Monday.

Tips and Tricks

Stay on track
  • Put the plan on your wall or phone lock screen.
  • Find one accountability partner and message each other daily.
  • Reward weekly wins with something small.
  • Do hard tasks first, when you feel fresh.
  • Review your Day 1 voice note on Day 30, 60 and 90 and notice the change.

Chapter Summary

Progress comes from steady action. The 30/60/90 plan builds foundation, then fluency, then performance. Track your days, review every Sunday and restart quickly if you miss. In the next chapter, we study the most common mistakes and how to fix them.

Key takeaways
  • Ninety days: Foundation, Fluency, Performance.
  • Practise daily and never miss twice.
  • Do a ten-minute Sunday Review.
  • Use checkpoints on Days 30, 60 and 90.
  • Find an accountability partner.
