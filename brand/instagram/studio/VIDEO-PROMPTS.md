# SpeakUp reel video prompts

## Join SpeakUp in Hindi (3 Gemini clips, about 30 sec)

Generate these one by one in Gemini (Veo). Each prompt repeats the same description of the person
so she looks the same in all 3 clips. Each clip ends where the next one starts.

### Clip 1 · The problem
A young Indian woman, about 24 years old, long black hair in a low ponytail, wearing a mustard yellow
kurta and small gold earrings, sits in a modern office meeting room with colleagues. Her manager asks
her a question. She freezes, looks down, and the camera slowly pushes in on her worried face. Cut to
her at home that evening, sitting at a wooden desk by a window with a green plant, sighing as she opens
her laptop. A warm Indian female voiceover says in Hindi: "मीटिंग में English बोलते वक़्त डर लगता है?
शब्द आते हैं, पर बोल नहीं पाते?" Soft, emotional background music. Photorealistic, natural candid
look, warm light, no on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos. Vertical 9:16.

### Clip 2 · Learning with a trainer
The same young Indian woman, about 24, long black hair in a low ponytail, mustard yellow kurta, small
gold earrings, sits at a wooden desk by a window with a green plant, wearing over-ear headphones in a
live online English class on her laptop. On the laptop screen, a friendly Indian female trainer in
her thirties, wearing a green kurta, smiles and says in Hindi: "घबराइए मत, गलती करो और सीखो।
चलिए साथ में बोलते हैं।" The young woman nervously says in English: "Good morning everyone, let me
introduce myself." Then she smiles with growing confidence. Slow camera move from over her shoulder to
her face. Upbeat, hopeful music begins. Photorealistic, warm evening light, no on-screen text,
no subtitles, no logos. Vertical 9:16.

### Clip 3 · Confidence and the call to action
The same young Indian woman, about 24, long black hair in a low ponytail, mustard yellow kurta, small
gold earrings, stands in the same modern office meeting room and speaks English confidently with an
open hand gesture while her colleagues nod and smile. Then she turns to the camera with a proud smile
and says in Hindi: "अब मैं बिना डरे English बोलती हूँ। आप भी Join कीजिए SpeakUp, ट्रेनर के साथ
English बोलना सीखिए।" Bright, uplifting music. Slow pull-back shot. Photorealistic, bright daylight,
subtle green accents in the room, no on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos. Vertical 9:16.

### In CapCut, after joining the 3 clips
- End card text (add it yourself, because AI misspells words): **Join SpeakUp** · English Speaking ·
  Learn with a trainer · Link in bio
- Put the SpeakUp logo in a corner for the whole video.
- Add Hindi captions with CapCut's "Auto captions".

Free AI video tools make short clips (about 5 to 10 seconds each), not one 30-second video.
So every reel below is 4 shots of about 8 seconds. Generate each shot, then join them in
CapCut or Instagram's reel editor (add text, music and the SpeakUp logo there).

Paste the STYLE line at the end of every shot prompt so all clips look like one reel.

STYLE: Photorealistic, natural candid look, warm daylight, modern Indian setting, subtle green
accents (a plant, a green notebook or mug), smooth slow camera movement, shallow depth of field,
no text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks, no brand names on devices. Vertical 9:16.

## Reel 1 · Join SpeakUp (about 32 sec)

1. Close-up of a young Indian woman at a desk in a bright Mumbai apartment, looking nervous,
   biting her lip as she glances at a laptop screen. Slow push-in on her face. Evening light.
2. She puts on over-ear headphones, takes a breath and starts speaking to the laptop during an
   online English class. The camera slowly circles to her side. A green notebook and chai on the desk.
3. Over-the-shoulder shot of the laptop: a friendly video class with four Indian learners in a grid.
   She laughs and nods, gaining confidence. Slow dolly-in.
4. She leans back in her chair, smiling proudly at the camera, and gives a small thumbs up.
   Golden light, slow pull-back. (Add "Join SpeakUp · link in bio" in the editor.)

## Reel 2 · Stop translating in your head (about 32 sec)

1. A young Indian man in an office meeting room freezes mid-sentence and looks up as if
   searching for words. Colleagues softly blurred. Slow push-in.
2. Close-up of his eyes looking up and to the side, thinking. A soft focus shift. Calm, quiet mood.
3. The same man at home in the evening, talking out loud to himself in a mirror while practising,
   with a relaxed smile. Handheld, natural feel.
4. Back in the meeting room, he speaks confidently with an open hand gesture as colleagues nod.
   Slow orbit around him.

## Reel 3 · Tell me about yourself (about 32 sec)

1. A young Indian graduate in a formal shirt sits outside an interview room in a modern office
   corridor, holding a resume folder, tapping a foot nervously. Slow push-in.
2. A door opens and a friendly interviewer waves them in. They stand up and straighten their shirt.
   Tracking shot from behind.
3. Inside a bright glass interview room, they answer with a confident smile and a calm hand gesture.
   The interviewer is seen softly from behind. Slow arc.
4. They walk out into the corridor, exhale, and break into a big happy smile. Slow pull-back.

## Reel 4 · One lesson a day (about 32 sec)

1. Morning: a college student in a hostel room opens a laptop on the bed, with earphones in,
   in soft sunrise light. Top-down shot slowly rotating.
2. A daytime local train in Mumbai: the same student listens on earphones and quietly repeats words,
   lips moving. Window light flickering.
3. Evening at a café: the student speaks to a friend across the table, both laughing.
   Warm bokeh lights. Slow push-in.
4. Night: the student closes the laptop, ticks a box in a green notebook and smiles. Desk lamp glow.
