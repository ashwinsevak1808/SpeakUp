## Tips & Grammar

**grammar-a-an · A or An?**
It is the sound that matters, not the letter.
an hour · a university · an MBA
Quick test: is it "a" or "an" European trip? Answer in the comments.

**grammar-since-for · Since or For?**
FOR + a length of time: for 3 years.
SINCE + a starting point: since 2023.
Write your own sentence in the comments and we will check it.

**grammar-have · Don't say this**
"I am having a car" is one of the most common English mistakes.
Owning something is a state, not an action, so we say: I have a car.
Follow for one fix every day.

**grammar-in-on-at · In, On or At?**
For time, go from big to small:
IN months and years · ON days and dates · AT clock times.
Save this for later.

**grammar-interview · Sound confident**
Four phrases that make you sound calm and confident in an interview. Practise them out loud today and save this for your next interview.
