# Image prompts: Indian learners studying English online with SpeakUp

Paste a prompt into ChatGPT (image), Gemini, Midjourney or Ideogram. Ask for **4:5 portrait** (or say "1080 x 1350").

## Rules that keep the photos looking like SpeakUp
Add this line to the end of every prompt:

> Natural, realistic photo, warm daylight, candid and genuine, modern Indian home or college setting, soft green accents in the room (plants, a green notebook or mug), clean uncluttered background, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand names on devices, shallow depth of field, shot on a 35mm lens.

- Never put text in the image; SpeakUp adds the words and logo.
- Keep the top third fairly empty (sky, wall or soft blur) so text can sit there if needed.
- If faces look strange, add: "natural hands with five fingers, realistic faces, no distortion".
